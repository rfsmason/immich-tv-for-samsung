Browse your Immich library from the sofa.

Your timeline, albums, people, Memories and favourites on the big screen, with photos, videos, Live Photos, slideshows and an ambient photo frame, all driven by the Samsung Smart Remote.

Project status Privacy model

Immich TV for Samsung is an independent, unofficial Samsung Tizen client for Immich. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Immich, FUTO or Samsung.

Why it exists

Immich deserves a TV experience that feels at home on the TV.

Samsung Tizen users often end up using browser workarounds, casting, or separate streaming devices to show a self-hosted photo library on the biggest screen in the house. This project keeps Immich as the backend and builds a focused, remote-first viewer for Samsung TVs.

What it does

Built into the app today

Everything below is built and connects read-only to your existing Immich server. Features marked New are complete but still being tested on a real TV.

Home

Rails for Recently Added, Favourites, Videos, People, Recently Viewed, Albums and Memories. Choose which ones appear in Settings.

Photos

Your whole library by date, including partners’ photos you show in your Immich timeline. Jump to any month, hold Up or Down to speed through the years, or press CH ▲/▼ to jump a year.

Albums

Browse your albums and open any of them.

Memories

Today’s “On this day” Memories from Immich.

People

Everyone Immich has recognised, and all their photos and videos.

Search

Find photos by people, a date range, type (photos, Live Photos or videos), a place from country down to city, and up to ten albums, using only the remote.

Favourites, Videos and Recently added

Each opens as a full view of its own, not just a row on Home. Photos, Favourites, Videos, a person’s photos, albums and search results can be shown newest or oldest first.

Viewer and slideshow

Photos, videos and Live Photos full screen, with optional date, time, camera details and face tags, and a slideshow every 5, 8, 12 or 20 seconds.

Video playback New

Skip through a video from its progress bar or with rewind and fast forward. With Autoplay videos on, slideshows play each video in full.

Ambient mode

A full-screen photo frame from all your photos, your favourites, recent photos, an album or Memories, with an optional clock. Start it from Settings or after the TV sits idle; any key takes you back.

Accessibility New

A high-contrast option with white text on black and a focus ring that shows on any photo, and a choice of when the playback buttons fade, or never.

Settings

Your server connection, and display, Home and ambient preferences.

Built for Samsung TVs

Not a wrapped desktop website.

01

Remote-first navigation

Directional focus, OK and Back reach everything. Play/Pause, rewind, fast forward and the channel keys are shortcuts.

02

Tested on a real TV

The main test device is a 2019 Samsung QE55Q950R running Tizen 5.0. The app is built for that generation’s browser engine, not a modern desktop browser.

03

Older hardware matters

The app avoids loading full libraries or full-resolution images into memory just because a desktop can.

04

Read-only by design

The app is for viewing. Uploading, editing, deleting and administration stay in Immich.

Privacy and self-hosting

Your photos stay yours.

The app talks directly from your TV to your own Immich server. Your photos and videos don’t pass through anyone else’s servers.

  • No telemetry or advertising is built into the app.
  • No subscription is currently planned.
  • Your Immich server remains the system of record for media, accounts, and access.
  • You connect with an Immich API key today; easier pairing with your phone is planned.
  • The API key is stored on the TV, so use a key that only has read access.

Availability

Not yet available to download.

There’s no public release yet. A Samsung store or beta release may follow once the app is ready.

What you’ll need

  • An Immich server running version 3.2.0 or later.
  • A 2019 or newer Samsung TV (Tizen 5.0 or later). So far the app has been tested on one model, a 2019 QE55Q950R.
